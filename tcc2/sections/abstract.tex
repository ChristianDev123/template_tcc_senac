\begin{resumo}[Abstract]
 \begin{otherlanguage*}{english}
    This work compares two processing methods for the automatic reading of conventional
    water meters in \gls{smart_metering} systems: \gls{edge_computing}, in which \gls{ocr}
    runs on the \gls{esp32cam} microcontroller itself, and \gls{cloud_computing}, in which
    the node sends the image and recognition is performed on a server. The motivation is the
    high cost of replacing conventional meters with smart models, which makes
    \textit{\gls{retrofitting}} a more viable alternative. Both flows use the same hardware,
    the same \gls{lorawan} link on the AU915 plan (SF8, 500~kHz, fixed channel), compliant
    with the 400~ms dwell time limit of ANATEL Act No.~14.448, and the same digit
    recognition model: a convolutional neural network trained on public datasets and on
    our own photos and quantized to 8-bit integers (INT8). In the cloud flow, the
    160$\times$120 pixel grayscale image is compressed to a JPEG of about 4~KB and sent in
    approximately 24 packets; in the edge flow, only the 9-byte reading is transmitted, in a
    single packet. Quantization reduced accuracy from 95.46\% to 95.38\% on the validation
    digits, a non-significant difference (McNemar test, $p = 0.29$), and the INT8 model
    recognized 97.7\% of the digits and 92.8\% of the complete readings of an independent
    test set. On the bench, OCR on the node took about 18~s, against 0.7~s to capture and
    compress the image in the cloud flow. Even so, the edge flow made the reading available
    on the server in 18.8~s, against 189~s for the cloud flow, and consumed 0.82~mAh per
    reading, against 4.64~mAh, that is, 5.6 times less. Delivery of complete readings was
    79.4\% at the edge and 66.7\% in the cloud, a non-significant difference with the
    samples obtained (Fisher's exact test, $p = 0.27$). We conclude that, in a low-rate
    network such as \gls{lorawan}, transmitting the image costs more than processing it on
    the node, which favors the edge in latency and energy, while the cloud makes the system
    easier to maintain and the model easier to update.
  \vspace{\onelineskip}
  \noindent
  \textbf{Keywords}: Smart Metering. Edge computing. ESP32. LoRaWAN. OCR.
 \end{otherlanguage*}
\end{resumo}
