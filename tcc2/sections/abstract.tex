\begin{resumo}[Abstract]
 \begin{otherlanguage*}{english}
    Most consumption meters in use, such as water, electricity and gas meters, are still
    conventional and require manual reading. Replacing them with smart meters is costly,
    which makes \textit{\gls{retrofitting}} a more viable alternative: a low-cost node with a
    camera photographs the dial of the existing meter, and an \gls{ocr} model converts the
    image into a reading, transmitted over a long-range, low-power network such as
    \gls{lorawan}. In this \gls{smart_metering} architecture, \gls{ocr} can run on the node
    itself, in \gls{edge_computing}, or on a server that receives the image, in
    \gls{cloud_computing}. This work compares these two approaches. Both flows were
    implemented on the same hardware, based on the \gls{esp32cam} microcontroller and the
    Radioenge RD49C \gls{lorawan} module, and on the same link on the AU915 plan, configured
    with SF8 and a fixed channel, compliant with the 400~ms dwell time limit of ANATEL Act
    No.~14.448. Recognition uses a digit classification convolutional neural network,
    trained on public datasets and on our own photos and quantized to 8-bit integers (INT8),
    with water meters as a case study. In the edge flow, the node runs \gls{ocr} and
    transmits only the reading, in a single packet; in the cloud flow, the node compresses
    the image to JPEG and transmits it split into several packets, and the same model runs
    on the server. The bench experiments evaluated the effect of quantization on accuracy,
    the computational cost of processing on the node, end-to-end latency, packet and
    reading delivery rates, and energy consumption per reading, measured with a current
    sensor, from which the node's autonomy was estimated. This work thus seeks to guide the
    choice between the two approaches in \gls{smart_metering} deployments, considering the
    \textit{trade-off} between reading accuracy, energy efficiency and system maintenance
    complexity.
  \vspace{\onelineskip}
  \noindent
  \textbf{Keywords}: Smart Metering. Edge computing. ESP32. LoRaWAN. OCR.
 \end{otherlanguage*}
\end{resumo}
